INTRO

What This Report Is

The Master Trader report is a full trading dashboard in one HTML page. It does five things:

One-page dashboard that outputs: (1) market context, (2) top picks, (3) ready plans, (4) risk/data checks, (5) hold/trim/exit for tracked symbols.

① Market conditionEvaluates the current regime before you look at individual stocks.
② Top opportunitiesSurfaces the highest-conviction picks from the full universe.
③ Ready trade plansEntry zone, stop loss, targets, and hold period — all pre-calculated.
④ Risk & confidenceData quality, execution confidence, and veto warnings for every setup.
⑤ Open-trade signalsHold / Trim / Exit guidance for positions you already hold.

SECTION 10

For stocks you already hold. This section tells you what to do with each open position.

Execution: EXIT → close; TRIM → reduce risk; HOLD → keep plan but monitor trigger notes.

Conf DecayWhether execution confidence has fallen since entry.
State ShiftA meaningful change in the signal regime since you entered.

How to act on each verdict

  • TRIM — Reduce risk: partially book profit or tighten your stop.
  • HOLD — Keep plan active. Continue monitoring next report.

WORKFLOW

Practical Daily Workflow

Follow these steps every day before placing any orders.

Daily loop: quality → regime → shortlist → View → execute → tracker.

1
Open the latest report

Always work from the most recent master_trader_report_YYYY-MM-DD.html.

2
Check Data Sources & Accuracy first

If data is stale or accuracy is weak, reduce risk or wait.

3
Read the Market Dashboard

Determine broad risk-on / risk-off tone before looking at individual stocks.

4
Build your shortlist

Start from the Hotlist, then review top rows in each plan section.

5
Open View for each candidate

Confirm: acceptable entry & stop · acceptable R:R · good data quality · no critical veto warnings.

6
Enter only when price is in entry zone

Do not chase. If price has moved away from the zone, wait.

7
Track open trades with Exit Signal Tracker

Act on HOLD / TRIM / EXIT verdicts immediately.

8
Review again after next report update

Signals and verdicts change. Repeat the workflow each session.

RULES

Risk Rules You Must Keep

These rules protect you when signals look exciting but the trade is actually unsafe. Treat them as non-negotiable.

Non-negotiables: stop first, no size-up on weak data, don’t force trades, obey TRIM/EXIT, cap per-trade risk.

✕
Never enter a trade without a stop loss in place.
✕
Never increase position size when data quality is weak.
✕
Avoid forcing trades when quality metrics are poor.
✕
Always respect EXIT and TRIM verdicts from the Exit Signal Tracker.
✕
Keep per-trade risk capped at your pre-defined plan limit.
📋
Important note: This report is a decision-support tool, not a guaranteed profit engine. Final execution responsibility always rests with the trader. Discipline and risk management matter more than any single score.
