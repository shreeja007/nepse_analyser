Key Data to Collect

Daily OHLC (Open, High, Low, Close)
Volume & Turnover
Daily price changes

Intraday snapshots (every 5-15 min)
Order book depth (buy/sell orders)
Top gainers/losers daily

Priority 3 (Nice to have):

Sector indices
Market breadth (advance/decline)
Floorsheet (all trades)
