📊 Volume Price Trend (VPT) Divergence

⚖️ ATR-Based Risk Management

Stop-loss = Entry − 1.5 × ATR(14). Target 1 = Entry + 2.0 × ATR. Target 2 = Entry + 3.5 × ATR. Position size = (1.5% × Equity) / Risk per share. Only signals with R:R ≥ 2.0 make the final watchlist.

📈 Bollinger Squeeze → Breakout

When bands contract to within 4% of price (squeeze), followed by a close above the upper band with volume ratio ≥ 1.5, it signals a high-probability trend initiation. This is the most reliable breakout pattern on NEPSE.

🎯 Fibonacci & Pivot Points
